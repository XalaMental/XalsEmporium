# Xal's Emporium - Changelog

## 1.1.0 - October 23, 2026

---

Housing decor is scattered across every corner of the game, and the game's own "where do I get this?" text is usually just a zone name, or nothing at all. I wanted a tracker that tells me exactly what I'm still missing and then walks me right to the vendor. So I went through the whole catalog, nearly 1,750 pieces, tracked down the real NPC for each one and the exact spot for almost all of them, and added a button that sets a waypoint straight to them. It also plays nicely with Xal's Keep if you're running the rest of the collection addons. Thank you for being part of the Xal family!

### 🆕 New
- Decor Collection window - browse every housing decor item by category, with what you own and what you're missing side by side. Search, "This zone only" and "Missing only" filters, and an option to remember them.
- Click any item for a "how to get it" card with the vendor's name, zone and a Wowhead link.
- Set Waypoint button - points you straight to the vendor, using TomTom if you have it and the game's own waypoint if not.
- Real vendor info for nearly 1,750 decor items, with exact map coordinates for 93% of them.
- A floating house icon appears when your zone has decor you're missing. Left-click shows what's there, right-click jumps to the closest one. Resize it or reset its position in Options.
- Minimap button showing your collected/total decor count, with an option to lock it in place.
- New settings window with a sidebar: Minimap, Zone Alert, Collection and Data pages, plus a Rescan now link.
- Works with Xal's Keep - when it's installed, the Keep's rook becomes the one icon and the collection opens inside it.
